## Before contacting support

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, Firstgram version, the steps that
caused the problem, the expected and actual result, and the approximate time of
the problem. Screenshots should not contain private photos, passwords, complete
payment details, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## Camera and Photos

Firstgram requires camera access for live camera guidance. Saving a captured
photo requires permission to add photos to your photo library. You can change
these permissions in iOS Settings. Camera features should be tested on a
physical iPhone; iOS Simulator does not provide the complete camera experience.

After you choose a reference photo, the controls beside the crop ratio let you
show its manual guide as a frame or silhouette (or hide it), turn face
detection on or off, and use AI alignment. Camera guidance runs on your
device; live camera frames are not uploaded to provide it.

## Account and social features

Firstgram supports accounts, profiles, posts, follows, Flowers, blocks, and
reports. You can browse public posts before signing in; publishing and other
account features require signing in. Your profile can show one optional link;
it is reviewed like other profile text. To report a profile link that breaks
the rules, email support with the profile ID. If Sign in with Apple or an account request fails, confirm that your
network connection is available and try again. For a moderation appeal, include
the account and content details needed to identify the decision without sending
your password.

## F Coin purchase problems

If an Apple purchase sheet did not appear, a purchase was cancelled, or your
F Coin balance did not update:

1. Confirm that you are signed in to the intended Firstgram account.
2. Confirm that the App Store purchase completed.
3. Reopen Firstgram while connected to the internet.
4. Do not repeat the purchase if Apple shows it as pending.
5. Contact support with the approximate purchase time and Apple product name.

Do not email complete card information or your Apple Account password.

## Refunds

Apple handles App Store payment refunds. Submit refund requests through
[Apple Report a Problem](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/). After Apple
approves or reverses a transaction, Firstgram may need time to receive and
process the updated transaction. A refunded F Coin purchase may reduce the
balance or create a debt if those F Coins were already spent.

## Creator Kit problems

If F Coin was deducted but a Creator Kit is not available, do not buy the same
Kit again. Reopen Firstgram, confirm that you are using the same account, and
contact support with the Kit name and approximate purchase time.

## Account deletion

Use the in-app account deletion control. External purchase-account deletion may
continue in the background when RevenueCat or Apple processing is pending. If
the App reports that deletion is still processing, reconnect to the internet
and try again later rather than creating a second deletion request.

Deleting a Firstgram account removes its active F Coin balance and Creator Kit
ownership. Creating a new account does not restore them. Apple and RevenueCat
may retain transaction records required for refunds, fraud prevention,
accounting, disputes, or law.

## Privacy and safety requests

The optional product analytics choice is separate from Content Safety Review. Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection on this device and requests removal of its pending queue, without preventing publishing. It does not confirm erasure of events already received by a provider. Signing out or uninstalling the App does not delete previously uploaded analytics. Contact support to request access, correction, cessation of processing, or deletion; we will verify the relevant identity and explain any outstanding provider processing. Please do not send passwords or payment credentials.

Email firstgram.official@gmail.com for a privacy request, content report, or moderation
appeal. We may request reasonable information to verify your identity or locate
the relevant record.
