# Firstgram Privacy Policy

Draft for review — not approved or published

**Effective Date:** August 2, 2026
**Last Updated:** August 10, 2026

Firstgram ("Firstgram," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information Firstgram processes, why we process it, when it may be shared, how long it is retained, and the choices available to you.

Firstgram is operated by **CHEN NING YU**. For purposes of applicable data protection law, the operator is the data controller, meaning the person or entity that decides why and how personal data is processed.

## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Firstgram iOS application and related services. It does not apply to third-party websites, applications, or services that have their own privacy policies.

## 2. Summary

- Firstgram processes live camera frames and pose-alignment information on your device to provide camera guidance.
- Firstgram does not request continuous or background location access. You may voluntarily attach a place name or map pin to a post.
- Account, profile, social, post, safety, and purchase information is stored using service providers described below.
- Photos posted with the **Public** setting enabled may be used by other Firstgram users as free camera templates.
- Profile and post content may be sent to OpenAI for content-safety moderation. Firstgram must provide notice and obtain your consent before this sharing occurs.
- Apple processes App Store payments. Firstgram does not receive your complete payment-card details.
- Firstgram does not sell or rent your personal data, does not display third-party behavioral advertising, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising.

## 3. Information We Process

### A. Camera, Photos, and On-Device Processing

With your permission, Firstgram may access:

- **Camera input:** Live camera frames used to align a person with a reference photo and provide pose guidance.
- **Pose and alignment information:** Human-body landmarks, outlines, or similar measurements generated from camera input to provide the alignment feature.
- **Selected reference photos:** Photos that you choose through Apple's photo picker for use as references.
- **Captured photos:** Photos that you take in Firstgram and choose to save or publish.

Live camera frames and pose-alignment information are processed on your device for the camera-guidance feature. They are not uploaded merely because you use that feature. A photo is uploaded only when you choose to use it for an account, profile, post, report, or another cloud-based feature.

If you allow Firstgram to add photos to Apple Photos, captured photos may be saved to your photo library. Photos stored in Apple Photos remain under your control and are retained until you delete them through Apple Photos or another authorized application.

### B. Account and Authentication Information

If you create an account or sign in, we may process:

- Your Firstgram and Supabase user identifiers;
- Your email address;
- Your name and email address supplied by Sign in with Apple, depending on what you choose to share;
- Authentication tokens, login status, and session information; and
- Records needed to secure your account and prevent unauthorized access.

### C. Profile, Posts, and Social Activity

We may process information that you provide or create, including:

- Username or handle, display name, biography, and profile photo;
- Photos, captions, and other post content;
- A place name, map location, or precise map pin that you voluntarily attach to a post;
- Follows, bookmarks, Flowers, blocks, reports, and related social interactions; and
- Post status, creation time, and other information needed to operate the service.

Your profile, posts, captions, attached locations, and some social activity may be visible to other Firstgram users or the public, depending on the feature and your settings. Your account email address is not displayed as public profile information.

The **Public** setting is enabled by default when you prepare a post. If you publish a photo with this setting enabled, other Firstgram users may use that photo as a free reference template while taking their own photos. If you later make the post private or delete it, Firstgram prevents new template access and removes Firstgram-managed cached copies from other users' devices the next time those devices synchronize. Photos that other users already created using the reference are not deleted.

### D. Content Moderation and Safety Information

To detect potentially unsafe, illegal, or prohibited content, Firstgram may process:

- Profile fields, profile images, post images, and captions;
- Automated moderation results and content-category indicators;
- User reports, blocks, and enforcement decisions; and
- Rate-limit, abuse-prevention, and security records.

When you submit profile or post content that requires moderation, the relevant text or image may be sent to OpenAI for automated content-safety analysis. Before this transmission, Firstgram will present a clear notice and request your consent. If you do not consent, you may continue using features that work entirely on your device, but you will not be able to submit content that requires cloud moderation.

Automated moderation may prevent content from being published or may flag it for further review. You may contact us if you believe a moderation decision was made in error.

### E. Location Information

Firstgram does not request automatic, continuous, or background access to your device's location. If you voluntarily search for or select a place, or place a precise pin on a map, Firstgram may store that location with your post and display it to others.

Map searches and map displays use Apple MapKit. Apple may process map requests, device information, IP address, and related technical information according to Apple's privacy terms.

### F. F Coin and Creator Kits

If you buy F Coin or use F Coin to purchase a Creator Kit:

- Apple processes your payment and payment credentials;
- Firstgram does not receive your complete credit-card, debit-card, or bank-account details;
- Firstgram and RevenueCat may process your App User ID, product identifier, transaction identifier, virtual-currency adjustment, purchase history, refund status, and related technical information;
- Firstgram may process your F Coin balance, Creator Kit ownership, orders, refund debt, Creator earnings, payout requests, and audit records; and
- Firstgram may store records needed to deliver purchases, process refunds, prevent duplicate credits, resolve disputes, and meet accounting or legal obligations.

### G. Technical, Support, and Security Information

Firstgram and its service providers may automatically process limited technical information such as IP address, device and operating-system information, app version, request timestamps, error information, and security events. If you contact us, we also process the information included in your support request.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide camera guidance and other on-device features;
- Create, authenticate, maintain, and secure accounts;
- Store and display profiles, posts, and social activity;
- Moderate content and protect users from abuse, fraud, and prohibited conduct;
- Process F Coin purchases, Creator Kit orders, refunds, Creator earnings, and payout requests;
- Respond to support, privacy, and legal requests;
- Diagnose errors and improve the reliability and safety of Firstgram; and
- Comply with law and enforce our terms and policies.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent, performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interests in operating and securing Firstgram, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time, but withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

## 5. How We Share Information

We share information only as needed for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including with:

- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, Apple Photos, MapKit, StoreKit, App Store payments, and platform security services. [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)
- **Supabase:** Account authentication, database hosting, file storage, and server-side functions. [Supabase Privacy Policy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **OpenAI:** Automated safety moderation of profile and post content after the required notice and consent. [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/)
- **RevenueCat:** F Coin purchases, virtual-currency transactions, refunds, and purchase-account deletion. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/)
- **Meta or Instagram:** If you open an Instagram link or interact with embedded Instagram content, Meta may process information under its own policy. [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/)
- **Authorities and professional advisers:** When reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate fraud or abuse, or establish and defend legal claims.
- **A successor organization:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of all or part of Firstgram, subject to appropriate confidentiality and privacy protections.

We require service providers that process personal data for Firstgram to protect it consistently with this Privacy Policy and applicable law. Third-party services may also process information under their own terms when they act independently from Firstgram.

Firstgram does not sell or rent personal data. Firstgram does not use third-party advertising SDKs or track users across other companies' apps and websites for targeted advertising.

## 6. Public Information

Information that you choose to publish may be copied, saved, shared, or viewed by other people. Even after you delete public content, copies may remain in screenshots, shares, search caches, or records controlled by other users or third parties. Do not publish information that you do not want others to see.

Public post photos are also available inside Firstgram as free camera templates. Making a post private or deleting it stops future Firstgram template access after synchronization, but does not remove photos that another user already created.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy:

- On-device app data remains until you remove it through the app, delete the app, or erase the device, depending on the feature.
- Photos saved to Apple Photos remain until you delete them from your photo library.
- Account, profile, post, and social data generally remains while your account or the relevant content exists.
- Revoked public-post template caches are removed from Firstgram-managed storage on other users' devices when those devices next synchronize.
- Uploaded staging files are removed after processing when the upload and publication flow completes.
- Moderation, report, fraud-prevention, security, and support records may be retained for a reasonable period needed to protect users, investigate abuse, resolve disputes, and comply with law.
- Purchase, refund, accounting, transaction, and audit records may be retained as required for fraud prevention, dispute resolution, tax, accounting, or legal compliance, even after account deletion. Where practical, these records are pseudonymized or de-identified so they are no longer directly linked to your active account.
- Residual copies may remain in encrypted backups until they are overwritten through our normal backup cycle.

Retention periods may vary according to the type of information, the sensitivity of the information, legal requirements, and whether the information is needed to resolve a dispute or enforce an agreement.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a portable copy of your personal data, and to withdraw consent.

You can:

- Change camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings;
- Choose whether to attach a location to a post;
- Turn off **Public** before publishing a post, or later make an eligible post private;
- Delete individual content where the app provides that control;
- Block or report other users;
- Delete your Firstgram account using the in-app account-deletion control; or
- Contact us at **yuchnng04@gmail.com** to make a privacy request or appeal a moderation decision.

When you delete your account, Firstgram deletes your authentication account, profile, uploaded profile media, authored posts and post media, F Coin balance, Creator Kit ownership, and associated social data from active systems. Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction records needed for refunds, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, accounting, or legal compliance. Creating another Firstgram account does not restore deleted F Coin or Creator Kit ownership.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request. We may deny or limit a request where permitted by law, including when information must be retained to protect users, complete a transaction, prevent fraud, or comply with a legal obligation.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. However, no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for protecting access to your device, email account, and Sign in with Apple credentials. Please contact us if you believe your Firstgram account has been compromised.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Firstgram and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live, including the United States and other locations where our providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers of personal data.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Firstgram is not directed to children under 13. If you are below the minimum age required to consent to data processing in your jurisdiction, you may use account or social features only with authorization from a parent or legal guardian where permitted by law.

If you believe that a child provided personal data without appropriate authorization, contact us at **yuchnng04@gmail.com**. We will investigate and delete the information where required.

If a change materially affects your rights, we will provide additional notice or request consent where required.
